Where to Stay in Taniti

Lodging Options

Lodging: Taniti has a wide variety of lodging that ranges from an inexpensive hostel to one large, four-star resort. There are many small, family-owned hotels and a growing number of bed and breakfasts. All types of lodging are strictly regulated and regularly inspected by the Tanitian government.

Hostel: The hostel is located in the town of Taniti, which is on the north coast of the island. It is a 10-minute walk from the beach and a 5-minute walk from the harbor. The hostel has a communal kitchen, a lounge area, and a rooftop terrace with views of the ocean. The hostel offers dormitory-style rooms and private rooms.

Four-Star Resort: The four-star resort is located on the south coast of the island, about a 30-minute drive from the town of Taniti. The resort has a private beach, a swimming pool, a spa, and several restaurants. The resort offers a variety of room types, including standard rooms, suites, and villas.

Family-Owned Hotels: There are many small, family-owned hotels located throughout the island. These hotels offer a more personalized experience and are often located in scenic areas. They typically have fewer amenities than the larger resorts, but they provide a unique opportunity to experience the local culture and hospitality.

Bed and Breakfasts: Bed and breakfasts are a popular lodging option on Taniti. They are often located in charming, historic buildings and offer a cozy and intimate atmosphere. Bed and breakfasts typically include breakfast in the room rate and may also offer additional amenities such as guided tours or cooking classes.
